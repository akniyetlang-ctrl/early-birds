Our meetups

What happens at an Early Birds meetup?

Three times a week we meet online, and every two weeks we get together in person. Join from anywhere, just bring your voice.

Tuesday · 15:00 · online

Everyday English ☕

Fun, everyday topics to get you chatting: movies, travel, food, habits and more.

Thursday · 15:00 · online

Health English 🌿

Conversations about wellbeing, motivation and taking care of yourself, all in English.

Saturday · 15:00 · online

Academic English 🎓

Level up for study and work: discussions, presenting ideas and speaking with structure.

🤝

Offline meetups, every 2 weeks

Once every two weeks we meet face to face to talk, play and connect. Dates and venue are announced on Instagram.

Questions?

Good to know

Can't find your answer? Send us a message on Instagram and we'll get back to you.

What level of English do I need?

Any level is welcome. If you can follow a simple conversation, you're ready. We'll help you with the rest.

Who can join?

Early Birds is a space created for women to talk, connect and grow. Everyone who comes with kindness and an open mind is welcome.

When and where do you meet?

Online three times a week at 15:00 on Zoom or Google Meet: Everyday English on Tuesday, Health English on Thursday and Academic English on Saturday. Every two weeks we also meet offline. We post each topic on Instagram beforehand.

How much does it cost?

The online membership is 6,000 ₸ per month and covers our online meetups. Offline meetups are 5,000 ₸ per meeting. See Pricing for details.

Do I need to prepare anything?

Nope! Just bring yourself and a bit of curiosity. We share the topic in advance if you'd like to think about it.

How do I sign up?

Send us a DM on Instagram @early_birds_sc and we'll save you a seat.
